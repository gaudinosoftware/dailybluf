Privacy

Your news stays on your iPhone.

DailyBLUF doesn't collect, share or sell personal information. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking.

Your stories and settings

The publications you add, the topics you follow or mute, your saved stories and your settings are stored on your device. DailyBLUF has no account and no servers of its own, so none of this is sent to us or anyone else.

News feeds and articles

To bring you the news, DailyBLUF connects directly from your device to the publications you add: it downloads their RSS feeds, story images and, to write your BLUF Report and explanations, the text of their articles. Like any visit to a website, those publishers' servers see your IP address and a standard browser identifier, under their own privacy policies. DailyBLUF doesn't send them anything about you.

When you open a story, it opens on the publisher's own website inside DailyBLUF, using Apple's Safari view. What you do on that page is between you and the publisher; DailyBLUF can't see it.

The BLUF Report and Explain

The BLUF Report, explanations and story summaries are written on your device by Apple Intelligence. The articles and summaries they're written from are read on your device and aren't stored or sent anywhere. The finished report is kept on your device so it doesn't need to be written again.

Your location

If you choose Use My Location, DailyBLUF asks for your location once to find your country and, for local news, your state or province and city. Apple's Maps service turns your location into those place names. DailyBLUF doesn't store your location, and your country is used only to suggest news sources and trending searches. You can turn location access off at any time in the Settings app.

Search and trending topics

When you search for news beyond your publications, your search words are sent to Google News to find matching stories. Trending Now loads Google Trends' public list of popular searches for your country. Both requests go to Google, which sees your IP address under its own privacy policy. Nothing else about you is sent.

Videos

Stories from YouTube channels play in YouTube's player, which is provided by Google and works under YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.

Paywall checks

When you open a story, DailyBLUF may load that page in the background to see whether the publisher keeps it for subscribers. Nothing is sent to the publisher except the normal page request.

Notifications, Siri and widgets

Report Briefing alerts are scheduled on your device. Siri shortcuts and the home screen widget work on your device and don't share your data with DailyBLUF.

Connectivity checks

When you're offline, DailyBLUF checks for a connection by loading Apple's standard connectivity page. Nothing about you is sent.

Subscriptions

DailyBLUF needs a subscription, which starts with a free week. Purchases are made through Apple, and DailyBLUF never sees your payment details. To check whether you're subscribed, DailyBLUF uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created for this app, your purchase records from Apple, and basic device information such as your iOS version, country and app version. It never receives your name, your email address, your publications or what you read. RevenueCat handles this under its own privacy policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings.

Analytics and tracking

DailyBLUF contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Subscription records are used only to open the app.

Children

DailyBLUF isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone.
