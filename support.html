Help with DailyBLUF.

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to gaudino.software@gmail.com.

How do I add a publication?

Go to Explore, tap the publications button, then Add Publication. Pick from the public feeds for your region, or paste a website or feed link.

What is the BLUF Report?

A short summary of Today's Bottom Line, written on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence from the stories you follow. A new edition comes each morning, afternoon, evening and late night, with a Weekly Recap on Sundays. It needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Why does a story say it may need a subscription?

Some publishers keep stories for subscribers. DailyBLUF lets you know, but you can still try to read it, or sign in with your subscription on the publisher's page.

How do I hide a topic?

Press and hold any story and choose Mute a Topic, or go to Settings and Muted Topics.

Can I ask Siri?

Yes: "Hey Siri, what's my BLUF?" opens your latest BLUF Report.
